Wix eCommerce, cart and checkout

Tiny Cart, rewritten

The pop-up a shopper sees right after "Add to cart" is the highest-intent moment in a store. It was barely designable and led nowhere. I rebuilt it as a surface that moves buyers to checkout.

The starting point: three ways in, one buried panel

Shoppers reached the Tiny Cart from the cart icon, a product page or a category page. But merchants could only customize it from deep inside the cart-icon settings, and only lightly: text, fonts, colors and the button style.

Worse, it had no path to checkout, and it showed too little (no discounts, no item details) to give a buyer confidence.

Benchmark: reassure, then move forward

I studied how leading retailers like Nike and American Eagle handle the add-to-cart moment. They share three things: a direct checkout button, a free-shipping nudge, and clear item details such as price, discount and variant. At this moment, the cart's job isn't to confirm the add. It's to move the buyer forward.

What changed for shoppers

  • A checkout button, not just "View cart."
  • Discounts and item details, so buyers see savings and confirm what they picked.
  • Two new slots merchants can use to extend the cart.

What changed for merchants

The Tiny Cart got its own page in the Wix Editor, with separate panels for design and customization instead of living inside the cart-icon settings. Merchants can also choose, per entry point, whether shoppers see the Side Cart or the Tiny Cart, and where the button leads: checkout, the cart page or express checkout.
